Section 41c of The Swedish Co-Determination ActVersion 2021:1114, English Translation

FredspliktLabour-stability obligations
41 c §Section 41c

En stridsåtgärd som vidtas i strid mot 15, 16 eller 19 § lagen (1999:678) om utstationering av arbetstagare är olovlig. Lag (2020:595).

Industrial action taken in breach of Section 15, 16 or 19 of the Posting of Workers Act (1999:678) is unlawful. (SFS 2020:595)
